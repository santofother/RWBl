## What exists now
- `CameraDirector`: on `phase_changed(RACE)` while the build camera is current, a `SwingCamera` smoothsteps from the
  build camera pose (FOV 45) onto the live race camera pose in `swing_time` (1.5 s, ends before GO at 3 s).
  Logs `[Game] CAMERA build | swing | race`. Starting straight in Race (`--phase=race`) cuts, logs `race` only.
  Other phases cut to the race camera.
- Spectator (no local chariot, or it is dead): ←/→ cycle chariots, logs `[Game] CAMERA spectate N`.
- `RaceCamera` now updates its pose every frame even when not current (so the swing can target it). Adds: sway
  (lateral slide + roll from `cart_angle`), boost pullback + FOV (`FLAG_BOOSTING` or BOOST effect), turn look-ahead,
  shake from `Effects.SHAKE` and from one-frame velocity jumps (works on clients).
- `CameraTuning` + `camera/default_tuning.tres`, registered with TuningPanel (F2) as "Camera". The old consts in
  `race_camera.gd` moved there (same default values).

## How to test
- `tools/tests/camera_test.sh` → `CAMERA TEST PASSED` (FOV unit check + standalone build→race log order).
- Manual: Godot → `game/match/match.tscn` → **F6**. Wait out Draft (10 s), click an item and place it in Build, wait
  or click **Done placing**: the view swings down behind your chariot before the countdown ends. Hold W: FOV widens
  and the camera pulls back on boost; drift sideways: camera slides and rolls. Ram a wall: short shake. F2 → Camera
  to tune. Die or have no chariot: ←/→ switch whose chariot you watch.
