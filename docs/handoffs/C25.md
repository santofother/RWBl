## What exists now
Defs `items/defs/<id>.tres`, scenes `items/scenes/<id>.tscn` + `.gd`, greybox primitives, auto-dealt by ItemDB.
- `bumper` (COMMON, obstacle, 3x3): red/gold pinball drum; shoves a touching chariot away from its centre
  (`bumper_push`, short stun `bumper_stun`); squashes on every peer. Ping-pongs chariots into each other.
- `lion_dung` (COMMON, terrain, 2x2): brown pile; slip kick + NO_STEER + DRUNK; one use per race (vanishes via
  `trigger()`, returns at race end). `dung_*` fields.
- `whirlwind` (COMMON, terrain, 4x4): translucent spinning funnel; hop + stun on entry, DRUNK and a sideways swirl
  while inside (`whirlwind_*`).
- `catapult` (UNCOMMON, obstacle, 3.5x4): wooden arm; lobs a grounded chariot up and forward (`catapult_up/forward`).
- `spike_log` (RARE, obstacle, 5.5x5.5): red/black spiked log sweeping round a post (angle = f(NetClock.now()),
  `spikelog_period`); contact = `kill(&"spikes", owner, uid)`.
None block the path. Tuning: new group "Chaos items (C25)" at the end of `item_tuning.gd` (defaults in script).

## How to test
- Headless: `tools/tests/items_test.sh` → `ITEMS PASSED`, `[Item] HIT <id> 0` for all five (also in `run_tests.sh`).
- Manual: host a game with bots (`--bots=3`), draft until a new card appears (use `--rounds` or play several rounds;
  rare spike_log is uncommon to see). In Build, place a card on the road (click road, left/right to rotate if
  needed). In Race drive through it: bumper pushes you sideways; dung = wobble and slip; whirlwind swirls you;
  catapult flings you ahead (land on rivals); spike log must be avoided by timing the gap.
