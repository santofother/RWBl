## What exists now
- `ScoreTable` (`scoring/score_table.gd`, `default_score_table.tres`, F2 panel "Score"): finish 5/3/2/1/0, trap +1 (cap 3),
  kill +2, self-kill -1, lap +1, clean lap +1, mercy +1, fewest deaths +2 (ties all get it), `non_trap_items` (speed_pad).
- `Scoring`: host collects events during the race, `score_round()` at `race_over` (fallback: at SCORE if the race timed out),
  RPC → every peer sets totals and emits `GameEvents.round_scored(round_points, totals)`. `[Score] ROUND r slot:pts`.
- Anti-grief: per item, `track.progress_at(item)` vs `race.progress_of(slot)` each physics tick for alive slots. An item pays
  trap points only if some chariot passed it alive **without having died to it** (covers "nobody passed" and "everyone who
  reached it died"). Otherwise `[Score] GRIEF item uid` and its hits count 0. Cap 3 applies after that.
- `ScoreScreen` (child of `scoring.tscn`, CanvasLayer 11): rows sorted by total: colour, name, finish, traps, kills, bonus,
  round, total. Shows in Phase.SCORE. `Scoring.get_breakdown()` feeds it.

## How to test

- Manual (user): Godot → `game/match/match.tscn` → **F6**; play the race to the end (or wait out the timer). After the
  race a dark table "Round 1 scores" appears for 10 s listing every player; check the numbers match what happened.

## Notes / design questions
- "Fewest deaths" with nobody dying gives everyone +2 (literal "ties all get it"). Say if all-tied should give 0.
- Mercy goes to the last entry of `finish_order` (a DNF counts) when there are 2+ players.
- A respawned victim who later passes the item does not make it "passable" (only clean passes do).
- Speed pad `report_hit` is ignored for points via `non_trap_items`; add ids there (F2) for other helper items.
