## Tuning fields and their feel (defaults, round 3: user said "cornering too easy", then "drifting loses momentum")
- Horses: thrust 10 (0→22 m/s ≈ 3 s), steer 1.9 rad/s (×0.55 at top), `steer_response` 5 = yaw inertia.
- Drift: grip swings the velocity toward the heading (speed kept) at up to `horse_max_lat_accel` 12; past that it
  slides and only loses `drift_scrub` 0.35/s × sin(slip). Cost of overcooking = a wide line, not lost speed.
- Cart: hitch 2.0 m, stiffness 6 / damping 1.2, `cart_max_lat_accel` 8 (fishtail), `cart_pull` 0.8 (sideways
  yank), `cart_drag` 0.15 (braking from a sliding cart), `hitch_yank` 1.5 (cart twists the horses → spin-outs).
- Flat out at 22 m/s for 2.5 s: ½ lock clean (30°/s); ¾ lock drifts 22° at 21 m/s; full lock slides 57°, keeps
  19.8 m/s; release → straight at 21.9 m/s within 2 s. Oval corners flat out need ≈⅝ lock.
- Oil (grip 0.05): the whole chariot slides. Whip: +8 m/s 0.8 s, cost 0.3, cd 0.6.
- Visual pull cues (all peers): horses lunge/dig in under acceleration, lean into turns. test_drive camera rides
