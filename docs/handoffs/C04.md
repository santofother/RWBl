## Tuning fields and their feel (defaults)
- Horses: thrust 18, max 22 m/s, steer 2.2 rad/s (×0.7 at top speed): full lock ≈ 90°/s, tight.
- Cart: hitch 2.0 m, stiffness 8 / damping 2 (cart swing period ≈ 2 s), `cart_max_lat_accel` 22 = the fishtail
  knob (lower = cart slides out sooner). Full lock at speed: horses slip ~13°, cart swings ~24°.
- Oil (grip 0.05): the whole chariot slides (slip ~85°) while the cart stays roughly in line.
- Whip: +8 m/s, 0.8 s, cost 0.3, cd 0.6; under 35 % stamina the boost and top speed drop; at 0 regen pauses 1.5 s.
