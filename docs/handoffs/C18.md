## What exists now
- `ScoreBars` (`scoring/score_bars.gd`): custom-drawn vertical chart, one column per player (colour + name + counting-up total under it),
  gold dashed **win line** at `win_score` (~85% height, y axis grows if someone is above), ghost ticks every 250, legend.
- Show (after `round_scored`, local only): bars sit at last round's totals, then **finish → kills → traps → bonus** grow for everyone at once
  (bouncy overshoot, squash/stretch, sparks, popping `+150`; a net-negative bonus shrinks the bar with a red flash and `−30`), columns **slide
  to the new order** with `▲ OVERTAKE!` on the climber, bars at/over the line glow and pulse, **MATCH POINT** flags, and on the winning round
  the winner's bar bounces past the line with a screen flash, confetti fountain and `WINS!`.
- Speeds itself up to end 0.4 s before `score_time` ends (never overruns). **Any key / click** skips (first 0.4 s ignored); **Tab** swaps to the
  old C11 breakdown table. `rows()` unchanged; new `bars()` (final order: slot, from, to, chunks) and `bars_done()`.
- `ScoreAnimTuning` + `default_score_anim.tres` (F2 panel "Score anim"): all timings, bounce/ease (`grow_trans` 10 = BACK, try 6 elastic / 9 bounce),
  pop size, sparks, confetti, line height, category colours.
- Logs: `[Score] BARS done` (finished or skipped); `[Score] BARS aborted` if the phase ended first.

## How to test
- `tools/tests/score_test.sh` → `SCORE TEST PASSED` (new `score_bars_test.tscn`: bars()/order/chunk sums/overtake/skip/win round inside a 3 s
  budget; plus `[Score] BARS done` in the real 4-bot round).
- Manual (user): F6 `game/match/match.tscn` with `-- --bots=3 --phase-time=20`, race to the end, watch the bars climb; Space/click skips,
  Tab shows the table. Then `-- --test-totals=0:900` (and again `0:1000` and win the race) → MATCH POINT flag / bar bursts past the line + WINS!.
- Look at it without playing: `$GODOT --path rwbl --resolution 1280x720 res://game/scoring/tests/score_bars_test.tscn -- --screenshot=/tmp/s`
  (windowed) saves frames of a normal round and a win round.
