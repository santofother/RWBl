## What exists now
- `combat/combat.gd`: rams (more mass × pre-impact speed along the impact wins by `win_margin`; loser's cart → wheel on
  the hit side, no wheels left → `kill(&"wheel", winner)`; loser's horses → stun 0.8 s; 0.5 s pair immunity), death
  forwarding, respawn (1.5 s, pit +1.0 s, 10 m behind the death spot, `race.notify_teleport`), crow, visuals.
- `combat/damage_fx.gd`: popped wheel = RigidBody3D copy of the wheel mesh, bounces 2.5 s then fades; death = confetti
  burst; dead chariots are hidden. Driven by snapshot state, so every peer sees them.
- `combat/combat_tuning.gd` + `default_tuning.tres` (F2 "Combat"). Chariot: dead chariots aren't solid (`_set_solid`).
- Crow: finishing the race, or dying with < 10 s of race left (no respawn), makes a crow until the next race. Every 8 s
  it drops a `mud_patch` (owner = crow, so it scores): human presses **action** (aims at the screen centre on the track),
  bot crows drop 25 m ahead of the leader. Crow drops are removed when the race phase ends.
## Public API
- `Combat.is_crow(slot)`, `crows()`, `crow_cooldown_left(slot)`, signal `crow_changed(slot, on)` (all peers; C13/C14).
- `request_crow_drop(pos)` (local), host `crow_drop(slot, pos) -> uid`, `set_crow(slot, on)`, `register_chariot`.
## How to test
- Headless: `tools/run_tests.sh combat` → `COMBAT TEST PASSED` (23 checks + clean 4-bot race).
- Manual: Godot → FileSystem → `game/match/match.tscn` → **F6**. Wait for the race, then ram a bot side-on into its
  cart (W + steer): a wheel flies off, it pulls to one side; ram again: it crawls; a third time: confetti, it's back
  ~1.5 s later. Hitting its horses only stuns it. Drive into a pit: back on the track in ~2.5 s. **F2 → Combat** to tune.
  Watch the combat test: open `game/combat/tests/combat_test.tscn` → **F6**.
