## What exists now
- `TrackPiece`: `_slopes(knots)` (straight ahead with height from a slope profile, exact heights) and `_banked_arc()` (tilt-in straight, tilted arc, tilt-out straight). New CONTRACT: first/last tangent level, height pieces end where they started.
- Pieces + cards: `hill` (`piece_hill`, common: up 4.5 m, level top, down; 104.6 m), `crest` (`piece_crest`, uncommon: ramp, sharp crest, drop, 30 m landing run; 81.9 m), `banked` (`piece_banked`, uncommon: 20 m tilt-in, 90° r 32 at 20° bank, 20 m tilt-out; R flips; 90.3 m). Shape knobs are `@export`s on the scripts.
- `Track`: `transform_at()` now returns the road frame everywhere (+Y = road normal), so items placed with it sit on slopes/banks. Road mesh split at the centre line. Non-pit tracks get a WORLD `RoadFloor` once the course has height (flat ground moved under the lowest edge). Bounds include height.
- `Chariot`: gravity always pulls; grounded wheels snap to the road and take its climb rate as vertical speed; on the road the wheels read the exact surface (`Track.road_ground`) because a banked road's triangles have kinks. Slope gravity along the heading (uphill slows, downhill speeds up past top speed, brake weaker downhill), bank grip bonus, crest launch when the road drops away > `launch_gap` (`[Chariot] LAUNCH`). Body stays level (respawn flattens a tilted transform); horse/cart models pitch and roll with the road (visual). Ground ray starts 2.5 m up so a road climbing past an airborne chariot still catches it.
- Bots: curvature ignores pitch, slow for crests (`crest_accel`), corner faster on banks (`bank_lat_bonus`) and hold `bank_inside` m inside.

## Public API
