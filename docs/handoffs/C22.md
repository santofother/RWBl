## What exists now
- `track/track.gd`: the course is a chain of TrackPiece scenes (next piece starts at the previous `end_transform()`), start line at the Track origin heading -Z, finish line + gate, walls capping both open ends. Self-crossing check = hash grid over the whole course (≈ 1–2 ms).
- `track/course_def.gd` (`CourseDef.pieces`) + `default_course.tres`: start, 2 straights, R, 2 straights, R, 2 straights, L, straight, L, straight, finish (424 m start → finish). `track.tscn` no longer has a hand-drawn curve.
- Pieces: `pieces/straight.gd` (`StraightPiece`: start 45 m, line at 30; finish 50 m, line at 12; straight 30 m), `pieces/corner.gd` (`CornerPiece`, r 25, 90°; corner_left/right). `TrackPiece` gained `end_transform()`, `road_length()`, `line_at`. Cards `piece_straight`, `piece_corner_left`, `piece_corner_right` (common); hairpin/chicane kept, `piece_length` = road length.
- `BuildPhase`: pieces go in at a joint; items after the joint move rigidly with the course (`[Build] PIECE moved`), items on a demolished piece / under the new road / on a now-invalid spot are removed (`PIECE displaced`). Demolishing a piece closes the gap (BLOCKS_PATH if that would cross the course). Free-build bots pick random valid joints.
- `BuildUI`: holding a piece shows a disc on every joint, the nearest lights up, ghost = new piece (bright) + whole new course (faint), green/red + reason. LMB always inserts at the lit joint; R flips (hairpin, chicane).
